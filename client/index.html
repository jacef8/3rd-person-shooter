How crews work

Crews are permanent: you stay in yours when you log off. The leader runs it; officers can invite and remove members. ★ The leader's B ping becomes the crew objective, and T Rally restores armor and gives +20% damage to everyone within 100 ft. Crewmates share XP (25% within 165 ft), see each other's tags and sensor reveals, and can't hurt each other.
